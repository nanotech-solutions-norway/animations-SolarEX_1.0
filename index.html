Dual-pathway coating logic

Quartz and Titan are intentionally separated

The animation distinguishes a passive SiO₂ easy-clean architecture from an active TiO₂ sunlight-supported hydrophilic / photocatalytic pathway.

Quartz · SiO₂

UV-independent hydrophobic / oleophobic behavior. Water beads and rolls off while dust, sand, salt, pollen and grime adhesion is reduced.

Titan · TiO₂

Sunlight-supported hydrophilic surface state. Water spreads into a thin sheet and supports easy-rinse, anti-fog and optical clarity visuals.
